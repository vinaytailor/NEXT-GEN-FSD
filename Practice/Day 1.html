What is a Compound?

When two or more elements are chemically combined, they form a compound. Compounds can be defined as substances of 2 or more different types of elements in a fixed ratio of there atoms. When the elements combine, some individual property of the elements is lost and the newly formed compound has new properties.

Chemical Formula: Compounds are represented by chemical formulas. A chemical formula is a symbols representation of the proportions of atoms that constitute a particular chemical compound.

The chemical formula of water is H2O which shows two atoms of hydrogen and one atom of oxygen have comebined to from one molecule of H2O. The chemical formula for common salt is NaCl which shows that one atom of sodium and one atom of chlorine have combined to form one molecule of NaCl.
